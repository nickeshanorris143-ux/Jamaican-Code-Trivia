Jamaican Code Trivia

What is the Jamaican National Dish?

Ackee and Saltfish.

Which year did Jamaica gain Independence?

The year 1962. Jamaica became Independent on August 6, 1962.

What is the Jamaican National tree?

Jamaica's National tree is the Big and mighty Blue Mahoe tree.

What is the Jamaican Motto?

The Jamaican Motto is "Out of Many, One People" which means that though we are all from different background and different races, we have come together to build one community of Proud Jamaicans.
